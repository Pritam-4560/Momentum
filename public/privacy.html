Privacy Policy

Application: Momentum (com.momentum.life.app) • Last Updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Momentum ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, handles, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

🔒 Offline-First Privacy: Momentum is built with a privacy-by-default architecture. Your habits, quests, journal entries, streaks, and personal goals are stored locally on your device and are never sold or sent to remote developer servers.

1. Information We Collect and Store

A. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is created when you use Momentum and stored exclusively on your device's local database:

  • Habits & Goals: Habit titles, categories, frequencies, reminder times, and completion logs.
  • Missions & Focus Data: Daily quest progress, focus timer durations, and productivity scores.
  • Zen Garden & Stats: Level, unlocked items, streaks, XP, and badge achievements.
  • User Preferences: Theme settings, sound/vibration toggles, and notification schedules.

B. Third-Party Services & Automated Data Collection

To provide advertising and support the free features of the app, Momentum integrates Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain standard telemetry and identifiers:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID) for serving contextual and personalized ads (where permitted).
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, OS version, carrier, network connection type, and ad performance analytics.
  • Location Information: General coarse location derived from IP address.

For more details on Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

2. Device Permissions Used

Momentum requests only the device permissions necessary to enable its features:

Permission Why It Is Needed
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers daily habit reminders and focus timer alerts (Android 13+).
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Triggers habit reminders accurately at your custom scheduled time.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restores your reminder schedules when the device reboots.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE Maintains continuous countdown and status for active focus timer sessions.
VIBRATE Provides haptic feedback upon completing habits or milestones.
INTERNET & NETWORK_STATE Allows Google AdMob to retrieve and display advertisements.

3. How We Use Data

Any data processed is used strictly to:

  • Deliver core habit tracking, reminder alarms, and gamified progress mechanics.
  • Display non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Ensure application stability and diagnose crashes.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your personal tracking data is stored locally on your device, you maintain full control over it at all times:

  • You can reset or wipe all stored data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Momentum > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all locally saved habit records and databases.

5. Children's Privacy

Momentum is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Security

Your habit tracking data is contained within Android's sandboxed application storage, protecting it from unauthorized access by other applications on your device.
